Solar Power Systems

Industrial & Commercial Clean Energy Solutions

Overview

Clean Energy Independence

As part of our commitment to delivering complete, reliable power portfolios, Trevor Technologies WLL provides advanced industrial and commercial solar power systems. Our solar solutions are engineered to help corporations reduce their reliance on conventional grid power, lower operating expenses, and meet aggressive environmental sustainability targets.

We combine high-efficiency solar panels with advanced inverters, hybrid battery storage systems, and seamless grid integration to build robust clean-energy solutions capable of performing under challenging weather conditions.

Our solar applications

Solar Solutions & Architecture

We design tailored solar configurations optimized for maximum yield and power availability across diverse facilities.

🏢

Industrial Rooftop Solar

Maximize unused rooftop space on factories, warehouses, and commercial complexes with high-performance, lightweight PV mounts for clean onsite generation.

🌾

Ground-Mounted Solar Farms

Scalable PV installations on open lands, complete with structural racking and smart trackers that align panels with the sun for maximum daily energy yield.

🔋

Hybrid Solar + Battery Storage

Store excess daytime solar generation in advanced battery banks (Lead-Acid, VRLA, Ni-Cd, or Lithium) to power critical loads during nights or grid outages.

🔌

Solar Integration with UPS Systems

We specialize in combining solar arrays with industrial AC/DC UPS systems. This ensures a multi-tier redundant architecture where solar power serves as the primary energy source, backed up by storage batteries, and secured by the UPS and utility grids for zero downtime.
