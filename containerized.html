Containerized Solutions

Mobile & Deployable Emergency Power Backup Systems

Overview

Emergency Deployments

At Trevor Technologies WLL, we possess extensive in-house expertise to custom design and integrate emergency backup solutions tailored to exact customer specifications. Our portfolio includes truck and trailer-mounted solutions supplied to various utility and oil & gas companies.

These deployable systems are invaluable during emergency grid failures, substation maintenance, plant turnarounds, and industrial startups, providing reliable and efficient backup power precisely when and where it is needed most.

✓
Fully integrated, turnkey, and self-contained systems
✓
Custom layout configurations inside ISO standard containers
✓
Designed for easy mobilization via truck, trailer, or crane
✓
Built to withstand severe climatic and physical stress
System Architecture

Key Features & capabilities

We engineer containerized enclosures that function as independent, reliable power plants in the field.

📐

Custom Engineered

Tailored layouts designed and integrated to meet user-specified power ratings, battery runtime, environmental, and connection requirements.

🚛

Truck & Trailer Mounts

Available as skid-mounted systems or integrated directly onto heavy-duty truck beds and multi-axle trailers for high-speed highway deployment.

⚡

Integrated AC & DC UPS

Equipped with complete online AC UPS systems, Thyristor/IGBT rectifiers, DC converters, and switchgear for comprehensive distribution.

🔋

Ventilated Battery Rooms

Dedicated climate-controlled battery racks, designed with ventilation and gas detection systems for Ni-Cd, Lead-Acid, or Lithium cells.

📦

Rugged Enclosures

Weather-resistant, insulated, soundproof, and sandstorm-proof ISO shipping containers with specialized HVAC and fire suppression systems.

⏱️

Rapid Commissioning

Plug-and-play connections (cam-lock terminal boards) allowing local operators to hook up and power critical loads in under 30 minutes.
