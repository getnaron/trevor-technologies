About Trevor Technologies

Since 2014, we have been dedicated to delivering reliable and advanced power solutions for industrial, commercial, telecom, healthcare, and critical infrastructure sectors worldwide.

Who We Are

Delivering Reliable Power Since 2014

Since its inception in 2014, our company has been dedicated to delivering reliable and advanced power solutions for industrial, commercial, telecom, healthcare, infrastructure, and critical application sectors. With a strong focus on quality, performance, and customer support, we provide complete solutions in Power Backup Systems, Power Quality Management, Central Battery Systems, and Solar Power Systems.

Our expertise covers a wide range of energy storage technologies including Ni-Cd Batteries, Lead Acid Batteries, Lithium Batteries, and Sodium Battery Systems. We offer end-to-end services including system design, supply, installation, testing & commissioning, retrofitting, maintenance, and comprehensive after-sales support.

Backed by technical expertise, industry experience, and a customer-centric approach, we are committed to delivering dependable, efficient, and future-ready power solutions tailored to our clients' operational requirements.

Complete power backup & quality management solutions
Expertise across Ni-Cd, Lead Acid, Lithium & Sodium technologies
End-to-end services from design to after-sales support
Headquartered in Manama, Bahrain — serving clients globally
