Industries We Serve

Protecting critical operations across diverse sectors worldwide

Proven Across Fields

Industrial Sectors

We engineer zero-downtime power infrastructure designed for the specific physical, electrical, and regulatory demands of each sector.

Power Generation Plants

Protecting high-voltage switchgear, turbine control systems, protection relays, and emergency communications in thermal, nuclear, and renewable generation facilities.

Electrical Substations

Ensuring continuous, reliable DC control supply for circuit breaker trip/close coils, protection systems, and telemetry/SCADA systems in transmission grids.

Oil & Gas Facilities

Delivering mission-critical backup power to offshore drilling rigs, extraction platforms, transport pipeline pumps, and refinery processes.

Petrochemical & Refining

Powering process control loops, emergency shutdown (ESD) systems, safety instrumentation, and alarm beacons in hazardous, volatile environments.

Water & Wastewater

Securing continuous power for massive pumping stations, treatment SCADA systems, remote flow valves, and chlorination processes.

Industrial Manufacturing

Preventing expensive product losses by backing up industrial automation controllers, industrial robotics, and continuous manufacturing assembly lines.

Data Centers & IT

Providing high-availability, double-conversion AC UPS and central battery storage banks to secure servers, cloud operations, and telecoms.

Telecommunications

Delivering compact, high-reliability DC power converters and batteries to telecom base stations, fiber nodes, and remote microwave exchanges.

Railway & Metro

Ensuring complete backup power for automated rail signaling, trackside communications, passenger safety displays, and emergency lighting.

Airports & Aviation

Securing air-traffic navigation aids, runway lighting control systems, baggage handling, and security check networks against utility transients.

Healthcare & Hospitals

Protecting critical life-support medical gear, surgical operating rooms, backup ICU grids, and temperature-controlled storage cells.

Commercial Buildings

Powering central fire protection systems, emergency elevators, corporate data centers, and secure financial trading environments.
