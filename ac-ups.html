AC UPS & Inverters

Industrial & Commercial UPS Systems — True online double-conversion VFI technology delivering complete power isolation for mission-critical operations.

Product Overview

Uninterrupted, Conditioned Power for Critical Loads

Our Industrial and Commercial UPS systems are engineered to provide uninterrupted, conditioned power to critical loads operating in demanding environments. Designed around true online double-conversion VFI technology, these systems deliver complete isolation from utility power disturbances, ensuring stable voltage and frequency for sensitive equipment and mission-critical processes.

Whether deployed in power plants, oil refineries, data centers, or healthcare facilities, our UPS solutions offer the highest levels of availability, protection, and efficiency — backed by advanced digital control and intelligent battery management.

Why Choose Our UPS

Key Features

Every aspect of our AC UPS systems is designed for maximum reliability, efficiency, and operational longevity in the toughest industrial environments.

📈

High Efficiency at Low Output

Maintains exceptional efficiency even at partial load conditions, reducing energy costs and heat dissipation across the operating range.

🛡️

Oversized Components

Higher reliability through oversized power components, designed to operate well within their thermal limits for extended operational life.

🧠

Redundant Controls

Dual independent microprocessor-based control systems provide redundant monitoring and protection against single points of failure.

🔌

Input Isolation Transformer

Galvanic isolation between input and output ensures complete protection from utility-side disturbances and harmonics.

⚡

High Short-Circuit Resistance

Robust design withstands high short-circuit currents, providing superior downstream protection for connected equipment.

🌀

Redundant Monitored Fans

Multiple cooling fans with real-time monitoring and automatic speed control ensure optimal thermal management and N+1 redundancy.

🔋

Intelligent Battery Management

Advanced battery charging algorithms with temperature compensation, automatic boost/float control, and comprehensive battery health monitoring.

🔄

All Battery Types Compatible

Supports Lead Acid, VRLA, Ni-Cd, and Lithium-ion batteries with configurable charging profiles optimized for each technology.

💻

Full Digital Control

DSP-based digital control provides precise regulation, comprehensive data logging, and advanced communication interfaces including SNMP and Modbus.
