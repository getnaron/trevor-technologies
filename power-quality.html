Power Quality Solutions

Advanced power quality solutions designed to enhance electrical performance, improve system reliability, and support efficient operation of critical power infrastructure.

System Optimization

Reliable Power Quality & Efficiency

Modern industrial facilities operate sophisticated automated equipment, variable speed drives, and non-linear loads that can degrade power quality across electrical distribution systems. Poor power quality introduces harmonic distortion, low power factor, voltage fluctuations, and thermal stress on electrical assets.

Trevor Technologies provides engineered Power Quality Solutions designed to stabilize facility electrical networks, mitigate harmonic disturbances, and optimize real-time power factor. Our solutions protect continuous production lines and mission-critical assets from unpredicted interruptions and costly operational downtime.

Built with modular, serviceable architectures, our systems seamlessly integrate into existing low- and medium-voltage switchgear installations.

Harmonic mitigation compliant with IEEE 519 standards
Dynamic and static power factor correction (PFC)
Voltage stabilization and reactive power compensation
Modular cabinet architecture for flexible scalability

Core Capabilities

Key Solution Pillars

Engineered systems addressing the primary electrical disturbances encountered in demanding commercial and industrial infrastructure.

Active Harmonic Filtering

Real-time compensation of non-linear current harmonics generated by variable frequency drives (VFDs), rectifiers, and industrial robotics, restoring clean sinusoidal waveforms to the grid.

Power Factor Correction

Fast-acting dynamic and static capacitor bank assemblies that optimize displacement power factor, eliminate utility penalty charges, and unlock spare transformer capacity.

Voltage Stabilization

Mitigation of sudden voltage sags, swells, and momentary interruptions, ensuring sensitive process controls and automation instrumentation operate without disruption.

Active Resonance Damping

Advanced control algorithms that prevent electrical resonance between installation capacitors and transformer inductances, preventing component overheating and failure.

Flicker & Imbalance Mitigation

Instantaneous single-phase and three-phase load balancing to eliminate neutral conductor overheating and stabilize phase voltage symmetry across the plant.

Real-time Power Analytics

Embedded diagnostic metering with communication interfaces for industrial SCADA and energy management systems, enabling proactive condition-based maintenance.
