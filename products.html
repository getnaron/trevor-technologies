Engineering Excellence

Product Technologies

Our power solutions leverage the most advanced conversion technologies available in the industry. Whether you need the robust reliability of Thyristor-based systems, the efficiency of IGBT technology, or the compact modularity of high-frequency SMPS — we match the right technology to your application.

Every system is engineered with redundancy, monitoring, and safety at its core, ensuring compliance with IEC, IEEE, and other international standards.

Thyristor / SCR

Proven technology for high-current applications. Extremely robust with excellent overload capacity and long operational life — ideal for heavy industrial environments.

🔋

IGBT Technology

High switching frequency for superior output quality. Low harmonic distortion with power factor correction and compact, efficient design for modern installations.

📡

High-Frequency SMPS

Ultra-compact modular units with highest efficiency ratings. Hot-swappable modules enable N+1 redundancy and maintenance without shutdown.

🔗

Hybrid Solutions

Combining multiple technologies and energy sources — solar, grid, battery, and generator — for optimized performance and energy cost reduction.
